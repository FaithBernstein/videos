About Faith

I made my first video in high school, advocating for bike lanes along our school's main road. It was a small project (with shaky footage and that all-too-familiar iMovie stock music) but it actually helped get the lanes built and showed me early on the power of video storytelling to make a difference in the real world.

Since then, I have been pitching, producing, and editing videos that have reached over a billion viewers. I have done editorial work at ABC News, Good Morning America, and Group Nine Media, along with branded storytelling for clients like Coach and New Balance. Across all of it, the goal has stayed the same: to make work that people feel, not just watch.

A growing focus of my work has been outdoor storytelling, especially running, endurance, and the human spirit that shows up in those environments. I am drawn to the moments where people push beyond what they thought they could do and how those experiences connect back to something universal.

I've worked across the entire lifecycle of production, in high-impact breaking news moments to artistic evergreen productions from pitch to publish, including all the coordination, communication, relationship building and technical execution needed to get it done. I like to nerd out on why something performed well, dissect the opening and momentum of a piece, and find a way to knit strategy and strong storytelling together.

Outside of work, I am usually outdoors with a camera in hand, hiking, running, rockhounding (ask me about this!), swimming in alpine lakes, biking around Seattle, or practicing on the pottery wheel.

The videos below were produced in the field or at the studio, working directly with subjects, with a few desk-produced documentaries in the mix. As producer, I handle everything from pre-production through final cut: researching and sourcing characters, coordinating gear, managing logistics, and setting the editorial and visual direction.

On location, I'm often behind a camera myself, or working closely with a DP. I ask the questions that get people to say the thing they've never said on camera, and I make sure we leave with everything we need for the edit. Most importantly, I prioritize the well-being of every subject, making sure they feel safe, supported, and heard throughout the process.

Many of these stories take on the biggest issues of our time, gun violence, abortion, climate change, racism, and try to make them feel personal.
